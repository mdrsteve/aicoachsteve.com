What you need before we start

A computer, and a little room to sit next to it. A Mac or a Windows PC. This does not work on a tablet or a Chromebook, because part of what I teach runs software that only exists on a real computer. If yours is a desktop rather than a laptop, we work wherever it lives, so it helps if there is space for two people to sit.

It almost certainly qualifies. You need macOS Big Sur or newer on a Mac, or Windows 10 or newer on a PC. That covers most computers from the last decade.

Not sure? Here is how to check, in about fifteen seconds.

On a Mac: click the Apple menu in the top left corner, then About This Mac.

On Windows: press the Windows key, type About your PC, and press Enter.

If the version you see is older than that, or you cannot find it at all, send me a message and we will sort it out. It is a two minute conversation and I would much rather have it now than on the day.

What you will actually be able to do

  • Hold a real conversation with an AI and get answers that fit your life, not generic ones.
  • Know when to trust it and when to check it. This is the part almost nobody learns, and it is the difference between a useful tool and an expensive mistake.
  • Ask questions about your own week. What is on my calendar, what is in my inbox, what should I be ready for.
  • Turn a pile of your own paperwork into something you can ask questions of. Appliance manuals, an insurance policy, a benefits handbook, decades of recipe cards. Ask the pile instead of digging through it.
  • Hand it a whole job and walk away while it works, then come back to something finished.
  • Make a picture you actually need. A sign, a flyer, a card, a logo for the thing you run on the side.
  • Set up something that runs on its own, on a schedule, and arrives without you asking. You will build one before I leave.
  • Know which tool to open for a given task, without asking anyone.

Your pages keep themselves up to date

Everything we cover, you keep. One written page per topic, with the exact button names, the steps in order, and what to do when something does not work.

These tools change constantly. Buttons move, things get renamed. Your pages are linked to my master copies, so when something moves and I fix it, your copy updates itself. No email asking you to download a new version. Nothing for you to do.

It is the one piece of this that keeps working on your behalf long after our last session.

Questions people ask

I am really not good with computers.

Then you are the person this was built for. If you can use email and a web browser, you have everything you need.

What if I do not know what I would use it for?

Most people do not, and there is a whole session built around exactly that. We have the AI interview you about your own week, and it comes back with ten specific suggestions drawn from your answers. That is usually the moment it clicks.

Is it safe to put my information in?

Three things stay out: passwords, bank or card numbers, and your Social Security number. Everything else is fair game, and using your real documents is what makes it useful. We cover this properly in the first fifteen minutes.

What if we do not finish everything?

Then we do not, and that is a normal outcome rather than a shortfall. You will have more written up in your folder than we covered, so there is always somewhere to go next. Extra hours are available if you want them and there is never any pressure.

Do you work remotely?

Not for this. All 4.5 hours are in person, because setup, installers and permission popups are miserable over a screen share.

Do I have to keep paying $20 a month forever?
